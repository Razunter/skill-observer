The form shows each proposal's target and gist, `remedy`, evidence and `preview`, so at Prepare the candidate's `gist`, `remedy` and `evidence` carry everything the user needs: scope and exact target (project root and relative file, or skill and provenance), the one mechanism and intended remedy, how it is verified (without claiming the checks passed), any missing evidence, that samples are reconstructed, and the combined evidence: source signal/entry ids with counts and short decisive quotes, User note, or the Later fingerprint. The form fixes the options: **Apply**, **Later** and **Decline**, none preselected. It shows the whole preview as wrapped text, marking indentation, repeated and trailing spaces `·` and tabs `→`.
