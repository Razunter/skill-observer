**A failed ledger recording.** When `observer_resolve` reports a ledger failure, no publication was undone. Report each Unrecorded publication with its target and the error, never as Later. Outcomes listed as having written nothing keep their outcome. Report them as unrecorded, not as publications. Report unknown recording as unknown, not as recorded or missing. If Resolve stopped before making any rule or amendment changes, report that it stopped. Do not call `observer_resolve` again for these outcomes, redo their changes or ask about them again. The user can inspect the ledger.
